# Gallery Hartaf

Paintings on a paper wall. The interface recedes. Colour lives in the canvases. Bronze appears only inside the wordmark.

## Tokens

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--paper` | `#f6f3ee` | Page |
| `--ink` | `#1c1916` | Text, the Enquire button |
| `--ink-soft` | `#5c564c` | Secondary lines: medium, size, captions |
| `--bronze` | `#7a5c3a` | The italic “all” and “art” in the wordmark |
| `--line` | `#e3dbd1` | Hairline under the header, above the footer |
| `--serif` | Cormorant Garamond | Titles and the wordmark |
| `--sans` | Jost | Everything else, at a light weight |

Bronze on paper is about 5.5:1. Soft ink is about 6.5:1. Both stay readable at the sizes they are set in.

## Wordmark

`G` `all` `ery` and `H` `art` `af`. The inner syllables are italic, a heavier weight, and bronze. The outer letters stay in the regular ink serif. It is the only place bronze is used.

## Layout

Left edge shared by the wordmark, the hero, and the text. The hero painting keeps its true ratio and sits in the first viewport, with paper beside it on a wide screen. Works hang in columns so a tall canvas does not open a hole next to a wide one. On an artwork page the title, the facts, and Enquire stay with the visitor while a tall painting scrolls.

Filters are ordinary links: All, Machines, Ports & Landscapes, Cities, Abstract, Portrait. They work without JavaScript.

One motion: the painting moves from the grid into its page. Nothing else animates.
